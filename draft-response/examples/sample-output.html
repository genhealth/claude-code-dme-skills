Coverage Outlook

The patient’s insurance on file is Blue Cross Blue Shield PPO (HIOPT plan). Most BCBS commercial policies cover L3020 only with a documented conservative-care trial and a clear biomechanical rationale for custom (vs. prefabricated) fabrication. If the patient is also Medicare-eligible, please note that Medicare does not cover L3020 as a standalone foot orthotic under Social Security Act §1862(a)(13)(C); it is payable only when integral to a covered AFO/KAFO leg brace or under the Therapeutic Shoes for Persons with Diabetes benefit. Neither path appears to apply here based on the clinical summary received.

Issues Identified in the Documentation Received

2 Prescriber on the SWO (Arjun Raj, DPM) differs from the clinician who completed and signed the clinical summary (Gregory House, MD). Payers require the face-to-face encounter to be performed (or co-signed) by the ordering provider listed on the SWO.
